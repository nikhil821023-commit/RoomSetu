FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is RoomSetu free?

Yes, signing up, getting matches and chatting are free to start.

Do I need a password?

No. You sign in with your phone number and a one-time code each time.

How does matching work?

You answer a few questions about your budget, locality and daily habits. We compare them with other people and show a compatibility score with a breakdown.

What does the verified badge mean?

It means the person verified a work or college email. It's a trust signal, not a background check, so always meet in a public place first.

Which cities do you cover?

You can search any locality in India. Results depend on how many people and rooms are listed in your area.

Can I delete my chats or block someone?

Yes. Deleting a chat removes it for you only, and you can block anyone from your profile.
